How talismans roll

A charm is turned into a talisman in one roll, the same way whether it came from a quest or the Melding Pot. Each of the four charms has its own skill tables and its own slot table.

  1. First skill. One entry of the charm's first-skill table, every entry equally likely. There are no weights. Its points are any value in the entry's range, each equally likely.
  2. Second skill or not. Mystery Charms never get one. Shining get one 65% of the time, Timeworn 75%, Enduring 85%.
  3. Second skill. One entry of the second-skill table, equally likely. If its range runs from negative to positive, a coin flip picks the side first: half the time it rolls 1 to the top, half the time the minimum to 0. A roll of 0 removes the skill. If it lands on the same skill as the first, the second skill is deleted, not rerolled.
  4. Skill strength. The game rates each skill against the best it could have rolled. A skill at its top value counts as fully strong; a negative second skill counts as nothing.
  5. Slots. The stronger the skills, the worse the slot odds. A talisman with both skills at their top value gets the worst slot odds the charm has.
  6. Which talisman. Strong skills and more slots give the higher name. Each charm has its own set: Pawn and Bishop, Knight and Rook, Queen to Dragon, Hero to Creator.

The Melding Pot

You trade 3 talismans and Wycademy Points for charms. Every melded charm rolls its second skill on the positive side only, so a melded talisman never has a negative or zero second skill.

  • Mystery, Luminous, Ancient, Fabled: 3 talismans of one band for 1 Mystery, Shining, Timeworn or Enduring Charm.
  • Halcyon: any 3 from Pawn to Dragon for 1 to 6 Mystery, Shining or Timeworn Charms.
  • Juju: any 3 from Pawn to Dragon for up to 2 Enduring Charms plus 1 or 2 Timeworn.

For Halcyon and Juju, higher talismans (up to Dragon) make more charms. Three Queen, King or Dragon Talismans make Juju give exactly 2 Enduring Charms every time.

The talismans are rolled the moment you place the order. The wait in quests only counts down; finishing a quest moves every order one step closer.

About

An unofficial, non-commercial fan reference for MHGU's talismans. Enter a talisman and it shows the exact chance of getting it from each charm and each Melding Pot style, and how that number is built.

Where the numbers come from: the game's own charm tables and the code that rolls them, read from the game files. The calculation here was checked against the game's own code, run step by step, before it was published. The odds are exact, not estimates from simulated rolls.

Development: Built by the author with substantial help from Claude Code, Anthropic's AI coding tool, reviewed and directed by the author.

Menu textures and font: carried over from the sibling MHGU apps.
